Our Story

From a small idea to a global bank

Founded in 2020, Stackly started with a simple belief: banking should be effortless, transparent and beautiful. Today, we serve over 2.4 million customers across 120 countries, managing more than $48 billion in assets.

Our team of 1,200+ engineers, designers and financial experts work tirelessly to build products that put you in control of your money — whether you're saving for your first home or managing a multinational business.
